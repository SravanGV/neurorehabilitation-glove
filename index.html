Core Technology

Four pillars of
neurorehabilitation.

01

Neural Signal Acquisition

High-fidelity EEG headset captures neural activity across 14 channels simultaneously, filtering noise at the hardware level.

02

AI Intent Decoding

Deep learning classifiers trained on motor imagery patterns decode voluntary movement intentions with over 92% accuracy in real time.

03

Soft Robotic Actuation

Tendon-driven mechanisms and pneumatic actuators deliver precise, natural finger movements with force feedback for safe therapeutic assistance.

04

Adaptive Rehabilitation

Cloud-based analytics continuously adapt therapy protocols to each patient's neuroplasticity, recovery trajectory, and performance data.
